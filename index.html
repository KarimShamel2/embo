Six years later

If I could tell you one thing…

I would choose the beginning again.

Not because everything was perfect, but because every imperfect moment led me here—to the person who has seen different versions of me and still stayed close enough to know my heart.

I would choose the growing.

We have changed. We have learned. We have had days that felt effortless and days that asked more from us. And somehow, growing beside you has made the journey more beautiful than I ever imagined.

I would choose the little things.

The conversations that lasted too long, the jokes that only make sense to us, the quiet moments, the looks across a room, the comfort of knowing you're there. Those are the pieces of our story I treasure most.

And I would choose the future.

No one can promise exactly what tomorrow will look like. But if I get a choice, I want more mornings with you, more adventures, more laughter, more photographs, more lessons, more ordinary Tuesdays—and more years of calling this love ours.
